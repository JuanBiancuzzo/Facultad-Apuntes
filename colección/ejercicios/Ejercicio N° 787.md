# Enunciado
---
Sea $D = \Set{ (x,~ y) \in \mathbb{R}^2 : x > |y|,~ x^2 + y^2 < 1 }$. Resolver $$ \begin{cases}
	\nabla^2 u(x,~ y) = 0 && \text{para} & (x,~ y) \in D \\
	u(x,~ y) = 1 && \text{para} & (x,~ y) \in \partial D ~ \text{ tal que } ~ x = |y| \\
	u(x,~ y) = 0 && \text{para} & (x,~ y) \in \partial D ~ \text{ tal que } ~ x^2 + y^2 = 1 \\
\end{cases} $$
